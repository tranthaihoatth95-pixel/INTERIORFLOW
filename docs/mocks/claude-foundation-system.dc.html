Foundation System Sheet

The base scale every surface is measured against. Six type roles, one spacing meaning-map, colour roles with a mandatory second channel, and an icon law strict enough that a reviewer can reject an icon with a ruler instead of an opinion. Every entry is a number, a token name, and a usage rule.

Why this sheet exists — measured, not opinion. Icons are mixed-source on 13 of 13 surfaces (Settings alone: 65 of 75 non-lucide). One screen carries 11 type sizes where the shell uses 4. Production carries 12 distinct icon stroke weights (2 · 1.8 · 1.75 · 2.2 · 1.9 · 1.7 · 1.5 · 3 · 2.5 · 1.85 …) and 12+ distinct icon sizes (13 · 14 · 15 · 12 · 11 · 16 · 18 · 10 · 22 · 17 · 20 · 26).

No guard could be written for any of it, because a guard cannot check conformance to a scale nobody has written. This is that scale. The machine-checkable rule list at the bottom is the contract for a future soi: guard — it is the most valuable thing in this file.

The boundary between technical and everything else

This is the reason a single scale failed. Technical is allowed line-height: 1.25 — tighter than any other role — because it carries no Vietnamese diacritics to stack. Ambient Home copy is the opposite job: it is read as language, and it needs air. The boundary is not taste, it is character content, and it is testable:

A text node may use line-height: 1.25 only if its content matches ^[0-9A-Za-z .,:×·°²³∅±≥≤%\/\-–—()"']+$ — digits, Latin letters without diacritics, separators, and the construction symbol set × · ° ² ³ ∅ ± ≥ ≤. Those symbols are not decoration: m² is the single most common technical string in the app (every BOQ row, every room area), and a character class that omits it would reject the very thing the role exists for. The instant a Vietnamese diacritic appears, the node is no longer technical: it is caption or body, and the 1.50 floor applies.

So 2400 × 750 mm is technical. Chiều cao 750 mm is not — it is a caption that happens to contain a number, and it gets 1.50.

Vietnamese constraints — already in force, restated so a guard can carry them.

① line-height must never fall below 1.50 for body, label, and caption. Diacritics stack above and below the x-height; below 1.50 the marks of one line collide with the ascenders of the next. ② ALL-CAPS is banned for Vietnamese running text — the diacritics carry meaning and uppercase forms lose or distort them. Uppercase is permitted only for Latin-only strings: table headers written in short Latin words, unit codes, revision letters, file extensions. ③ Negative tracking is banned for Vietnamese running text — tightening pulls the mark of one glyph over its neighbour. Negative tracking survives only at display and title, where size gives the marks room.

05 Iconography grammar

The centrepiece. This is the section that ends the 13/13 mixing, and it is written so a reviewer rejects an icon by measurement, not by taste.

Is lucide the system, or is it being replaced? — lucide IS the system.

Measured: lucide-react is imported in 214 files. Replacing it means redrawing several hundred glyphs and touching 214 files, which buys nothing a constraint cannot buy. The problem was never the source of the icons — it was that nothing constrained how they were used. Production carries 12 distinct stroke weights and 12+ distinct sizes; that is the actual defect, and it is a defect lucide did not cause.

So: lucide is the canonical set, wrapped and constrained. The 65-of-75 non-lucide glyphs in Settings and their siblings on the other 12 surfaces are the ones that must go — either replaced by the lucide equivalent, or, where no equivalent exists (nghề symbols: tường, cửa, cao độ, trục, mặt cắt), redrawn to the grammar below and admitted to a named house set. Nothing enters the app outside those two sets.

The grammar — every clause measurable

G3 hard limits — measurable, because this is where it always goes wrong. scale ≤ 1.015. blur ≤ 24px. Zero box-shadow with a positive spread. Zero filter: drop-shadow carrying a hue. Zero outer glow, halo, aura, caustic ring, or bloom. The only shadows permitted on G3 are the two inset hairlines that describe the edge, plus --shadow-node.

G3 budget: at most 3 instances per screen. The chốt reserves it for Vào xưởng, the selected segmented lens, and at most one or two signature action states. G3 everywhere collapses the whole ladder — scarcity is the entire value.

Contradiction found and bridged. LUAT-VAT-LIEU-KINH-G0-G3.md speaks in G0/G1/G2/G3. globals.css contains zero occurrences of those names — it speaks in --kinh-mong / --kinh-vua / --kinh-dac and --vl-dac / --vl-gan-dac. Two vocabularies for one ladder, sharing no word. The table above is the bridge, and it is the whole reason a reviewer could not previously tell whether a surface complied: the law and the tokens were not speaking the same language.

No new colour tokens are added. G0–G3 map onto tokens that already exist. What is missing is a name, and names cost nothing: --g0 … --g3 should be declared as aliases so the law and the code finally use one word.

Nesting law, restated: glass never sits on glass. A floating glass panel portals to the document root rather than nesting inside glass chrome — the two backdrop filters compose into a wash and the inner surface goes transparent. Where depth is needed and content must stay sharp, the legal form is glass shell + near-opaque core (--vl-gan-dac, blur --vl-gan-dac-nhoe 6px).

09 Machine-checkable rule list

The contract for a future soi:foundation guard. Each rule is phrased so a script can test it against source, with no judgement call.

  1. Type — size closure. No text node may compute a font-size outside {12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 20, 28}px. 15 is legal only inside (hover:none) and (pointer:coarse). Any literal font-size in .tsx/.css not equal to one of --fs-xs · --fs-ui · --fs-sm · --fs-md · --fs-lg · --fs-xl fails.
  2. Type — token, not literal. No font-size declaration may use a raw px value. It must be var(--fs-*). Count of raw declarations must be 0.
  3. Type — --fs-2xs is retired. Occurrences of --fs-2xs must be 0.
  4. Type — Vietnamese line-height floor. Any rule with line-height < 1.5 must apply only to selectors annotated /* technical */, and the corresponding string must match ^[0-9A-Za-z .,:×·°²³∅±≥≤%\/\-–—()"']+$. A sub-1.5 line-height on a string containing any of ăâđêôơư or any combining diacritic fails.
  5. Type — no ALL-CAPS Vietnamese. No element with text-transform: uppercase may contain a string with a Vietnamese diacritic. Literal upper-case Vietnamese strings in source fail equally.
  6. Type — no negative tracking on running text. letter-spacing < 0 is legal only where computed font-size ≥ 20px. The body{} rule must not carry a negative value.
  7. Type — font fallback. Every font-family containing var( must supply a fallback inside the parentheses. var(--font-sans) with no second argument fails.
  8. Spacing — step closure. Every margin, padding, gap, top/right/bottom/left px literal must be in {0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64}. 80 fails. 1px is exempt (hairline).
  9. Radius — step closure. Every border-radius must resolve to one of --r-1 · --r-2 · --r-3 · --r-4 · --r-full, or to max(4px, calc(var(--r-*) - Npx)) with N ≤ 8. Any other literal fails.
  10. Radius — alias retirement. Occurrences of --radius-sm · --radius-md · --radius-lg · --radius-xl · --radius must trend to 0 (baseline 62; the guard fails on any increase).
  11. Icon — single source. Every icon element must originate from lucide-react or from the named house set. Any inline <svg> outside those two, in a component file, fails.
  12. Icon — stroke weight. Every icon must render stroke-width="1.5". Any strokeWidth prop ≠ 1.5 fails. Baseline: 12 distinct values, ~120 occurrences.
  13. Icon — optical size. Every icon size must be in {14, 16, 18, 20}. Baseline: 12+ distinct values; 13/15/12/11/10/22/17/26 all fail.
  14. Icon — grid. Every icon viewBox must equal "0 0 24 24".
  15. Icon — terminals and joins. Every icon must carry stroke-linecap="round" and stroke-linejoin="round".
  16. Icon — colour. Every icon must use stroke="currentColor". Any hard-coded hex in an icon stroke or fill fails.
  17. Icon — fill law. A filled icon (fill ≠ "none") must sit inside an element carrying a selected/on marker (aria-selected, aria-pressed="true", or data-selected). A filled icon in a resting control fails.
  18. Icon — detail budget. House-set glyphs must contain ≤ 5 drawing children.
  19. Colour — no literals. Outside the single marked token-mirror block, no #rrggbb or rgb() literal may appear in any .tsx, .css, or .dc.html.
  20. Colour — --t5 is not text. No color declaration may resolve to --t5.
  21. Colour — accent is not text. color: var(--accent) is legal only where computed font-size ≥ 18.66px and font-weight ≥ 600.
  22. Colour — warning placement. color: var(--warning) must not appear on an element whose computed background resolves to --bg.
  23. Colour — tertiary on glass. color: var(--t3) must not appear on any element carrying backdrop-filter or a --kinh-* background. Use --kinh-chu-phu.
  24. Colour — second channel. Every element whose only distinguishing property is --danger, --warning, or --success must also contain a text node or an icon child. A colour-only status indicator fails.
  25. Focus — never suppressed. outline: none is legal only when the same rule set defines a :focus-visible ring. Unpaired outline: none fails.
  26. Hit region. Every interactive element must compute ≥ 32 × 32 on desktop and ≥ 44 × 44 under coarse pointer.
  27. Rail. The sidebar rail's first nấc must compute exactly 52px. Any other value fails.
  28. Material — no glass on glass. No element with backdrop-filter may have an ancestor with backdrop-filter inside the same stacking context.
  29. Material — G3 scarcity. At most 3 elements per rendered screen may carry --kinh-dac together with a scale transform.
  30. Material — G3 has no aura. No element carrying --kinh-dac may declare a box-shadow with positive spread, a filter: drop-shadow with a hue, or any non-inset shadow other than --shadow-node.
  31. Material — G3 bulge ceiling. Any scale() on a --kinh-dac element must be ≤ 1.015.
  32. Material — blur ceiling. No backdrop-filter: blur() may exceed 24px.
  33. Elevation — closure. Every box-shadow must be --shadow-node, --shadow-pop, --shadow-sheet, or an inset hairline. Bespoke shadow literals fail.
  34. Motion — duration closure. Every transition-duration and animation-duration must be one of the five --nhip-* tokens.
  35. Motion — single curve. Every transition-timing-function must be var(--ease-apple) or a linear ramp for a progress indicator.
  36. Motion — reduced-motion coverage. Every rule declaring animation with infinite must have a matching prefers-reduced-motion: reduce override that sets a static state, not a longer duration.
  37. Density — token closure. Only --tap, --row, --gap, --pad-card, --fs-ui may be redefined inside (hover:none) and (pointer:coarse). Any other token redefined there fails.
  38. Density — hover is never sole. Every element whose content appears only on :hover must also expose it on :focus-visible and on press.
  39. Theme — attribute driven. Theme-varying declarations must sit under :root[data-theme='light'] or :root[data-theme='dark']. A colour token defined only inside prefers-color-scheme fails.
  40. Disabled — reachable reason. Every element carrying --mo-vo-hieu must also carry aria-disabled="true" and aria-describedby. The bare disabled attribute and title= both fail.
